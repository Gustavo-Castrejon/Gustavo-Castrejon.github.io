About Me

Hola, I'm Gustavo Castrejon, a first-generation Mexican-American engineering student at UCLA expecting to graduate soon, with experience across both hardware and software. My interest in engineering started young, taking things apart, fixing electronics, and learning hands-on skills from my abuelo. Over time, that early curiosity grew into a passion for solving problems, designing systems, and building things that make a real impact.

I've worked on projects involving circuit design, prototyping, software development, and automation through my recent work experience. I enjoy roles that challenge me, push me to learn, and let me apply both sides of my engineering background.

As I approach graduation, I'm exploring a wide range of opportunities across engineering and keeping an open mind about where I can contribute the most. Thank you for checking out my portfolio. I'm always happy to connect.
